    What actually happens at 2 AM

    Nothing reaches 2:00. As daylight time ends, 1:59:59 AM EDT is followed by 1:00:00 AM EST. The hour hand, which had almost reached the 2, is set back onto the 1, and the same sixty minutes run again under a new offset, UTC−5 instead of UTC−4.

    The extra hour is real sleep for most people, and a genuine headache for anything that schedules, bills or logs by local time, because one clock reading now names two different moments.

    And in the spring

    The mirror image comes on Sunday 14 March 2027, when 2:00 AM EST is skipped entirely and the clock springs forward to 3:00 AM EDT. That night an hour goes missing; tonight, one is given back.

    Standard time then holds until that morning. Tonight is the trade settling: the hour borrowed in March is repaid, with the whole repeated hour spent between 1 and 2 AM.
